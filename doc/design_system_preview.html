Properties of Water Essential for Life

Source: NIE Biology Resource Book (Unit 2, Chapter 2.1)

1. High Specific Heat Capacity: Water absorbs or loses a large amount of heat energy with minimal temperature change. This buffers aquatic environments against extreme temperature fluctuations.

2. High Latent Heat of Vaporization: Evaporative cooling (e.g. sweating/transpiration) removes heat effectively due to strong hydrogen bonding between molecules.

3. Cohesion and Surface Tension: Hydrogen bonding creates high cohesive forces among water molecules, aiding xylem transport in plants.
